## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on developing an interactive sales and profitability analytics dashboard using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard provides an executive-level view of business performance and allows users to analyze:

* Overall sales performance
* Total profit and profit margin
* Product-level profitability
* Loss-making products
* Customer segment margins
* Category-wise sales and profit
* Monthly sales and profit trends
* State-wise sales performance
* On-time delivery performance

The objective is to convert raw business data into an interactive reporting solution that can support **data-driven business decisions**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:

1. What are the total sales and total profit?
2. What is the overall profit margin?
3. Which product categories generate the highest sales and profit?
4. Which customer segments have better margins?
5. Which products are generating losses?
6. How are sales and profit changing over time?
7. Which states contribute the most to sales?
8. What is the current on-time delivery performance?
9. Which products require further profitability investigation?

# 🚀 How to Use

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the `PowerBI` folder.
3. Open `Sales_Profitability_Dashboard.pbix` using **Power BI Desktop**.
4. If required, update the data source path.
5. Refresh the dataset.
6. Use the available filters and slicers.
7. Navigate through the dashboard pages to explore sales, profitability, products, trends, and geographic performance.

The live dashboard provides an interactive experience where users can apply filters and explore the available business metrics.
