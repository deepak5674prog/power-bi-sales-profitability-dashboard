# 📊 Sales & Profitability Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI business intelligence dashboard** designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer segments, delivery efficiency, monthly trends, and geographic sales.

The project transforms business data into actionable insights using **Power Query, DAX, and interactive Power BI visualizations**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to build an interactive analytics dashboard that helps businesses understand:

* Overall sales and profitability
* Product-level performance
* Loss-making products
* Customer segment profitability
* Monthly sales and profit trends
* Category-wise performance
* Delivery efficiency
* State-wise sales performance

The dashboard provides a centralized view of important business KPIs and enables users to interact with the data using filters and slicers.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was developed to answer key business questions such as:

1. What is the overall sales and profit performance?
2. What is the company's overall profit margin?
3. Which product categories generate the highest sales and profit?
4. Which customer segments have better profit margins?
5. Which products are generating losses?
6. How are sales and profit changing month-over-month?
7. Which states contribute the most to sales?
8. What is the current on-time delivery performance?
9. How can product-level profitability be monitored?

# 🔍 Key Insights

### Sales Performance

The dashboard records approximately **47.85M in Total Net Sales** with **5.60M in Total Profit**, resulting in an overall **11.7% profit margin**.

### Category Performance

Sales and profit were compared across major product categories to understand which categories contribute most significantly to overall business performance.

### Customer Segment Analysis

Margin performance was analyzed across:

* Consumer
* Corporate
* Home Office

This allows businesses to compare the profitability of different customer segments.

### Product Profitability

Product-level analysis revealed **179 loss-making products**. These products can be further investigated for pricing, discounting, operational costs, or inventory decisions.

### Sales Trends

Monthly sales and profit trends were analyzed across **2024 and 2025** to identify changes in business performance over time.

### Geographic Performance

State-wise sales were visualized geographically to identify regions contributing to overall sales.

### Delivery Performance

The dashboard tracks an **On-Time Delivery Rate of 43.5%**, providing a KPI for monitoring delivery efficiency.

# 🚀 How to Use the Project

6. Navigate between dashboard pages to analyze sales, profitability, products, trends, and geography.
